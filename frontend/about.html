About Ganak Calculator

Ganak Calculator is an independent calculation workspace created by Shubhankar Sinha.

The project brings several useful tools into one place, including a scientific calculator, equation solver, graphing calculator, calculus tools, matrix operations, statistics, probability, unit conversions, finance calculations, discount and GST calculations, BMI, and date calculations.

The goal is to make common calculations easier to access through a clear, practical interface for learning, exploration, and everyday problem-solving. Results should be checked when they are used for important academic, financial, health, or professional decisions.
